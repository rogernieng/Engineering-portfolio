G-Class Motor - Model Rocket

Overview

Co-engineered with a team of five to design, fabricate, simulate, and launch a G-Class model rocket. The goal of this project is to design, simulate, and fabricate a rocket from scratch that utilizes motor from G-class

Project Workflow

The rocket was developed through an iterative design process involving CAD modeling, flight simulation, fabrication, and physical assembly.

  • Designed and developed a model rocket from the ground up.
  • Used CAD to create and refine the rocket's components with SOLIDWORKS.
  • Simulated the rocket's flight performance using OpenRocket to evaluate stability and expected performance before launch.
  • Considered center of mass and aerodynamic stability during the design process.
  • Iterated component designs based on simulation results and manufacturing constraints.
  • Manufactured Fin can and nosecone using 3d printed
  • Assembled through friction fitted and heat pressed tested the rocket as a five-person engineering team.
  • Launched the rocket

Tools & Technologies

  • SOLIDWORKS: CAD modeling and component design
  • OpenRocket: Flight simulation and stability analysis
  • 3D Printing: PLA print and fabrication tolerance.
  • Avionics: Arduino Nano, BMP280 , Adafruit Accelerometer/Gyroscope, 9V battery, SD card, Camera.

Results

  • Rocket crashed due to miscalculation of actual Center of Pressure and mass
  • Used OpenRocket simulation to evaluate the rocket's flight trajectory but failed to acknowledge the actual center of mass instead relied solely on theoritical
  • Obtain camera footage of the flight
  • Gained hands-on experience taking an engineering concept from CAD and simulation through fabrication and testing.
  • Learned the importance of actualenvironmental features vs simulated
