H-Class Rocket Kit

Overview

Designed, manufactured, and prepared a high-power model rocket for an L1 rocketry certification attempt using the Zephyr rocket kit and an H283ST-14A motor. The project focused on flight simulation, rocket stability, mechanical assembly, motor configuration, and launch preparation.

Project Workflow

The rocket was developed through a hands-on engineering process combining flight simulation, structural assembly, fabrication, motor configuration, and launch preparation.

  • Studied the structural components and construction requirements of a high-power model rocket.
  • Used OpenRocket to simulate the rocket's expected flight trajectory and evaluate stability and motor performance.
  • Evaluated the rocket's center of mass, center of pressure, and stability margin prior to launch.
  • Manufactured and assembled the rocket using the Zephyr rocket kit, including the airframe, fins, motor mount, and recovery components.
  • Configured the motor delay using a Universal Delay Drill Tool (UDDT) based on the simulated flight profile.
  • Performed final assembly and pre-launch checks to verify the rocket was prepared for flight.
  • Launched the completed rocket as part of an L1 certification attempt.

Tools & Technologies

  • OpenRocket: Flight simulation, trajectory analysis, and stability evaluation.
  • UDDT: Motor delay configuration and preparation.
  • Mechanical Fabrication: Rocket assembly, component preparation, alignment, and structural assembly.
  • High-Power Rocketry: H-class motor configuration and L1 certification preparation.

Engineering Analysis

Flight performance was evaluated in OpenRocket before launch to understand the relationship between the rocket's mass, aerodynamic stability, and motor performance.

  • Analyzed center of mass and center of pressure to evaluate aerodynamic stability.
  • Used simulated flight performance to determine an appropriate motor configuration and delay.
  • Evaluated the expected flight trajectory before committing to the physical launch.
  • Applied simulation results to physical rocket preparation and motor configuration.

Results

  • Completed the construction and flight preparation of the high-power rocket.
  • Successfully applied OpenRocket simulation to evaluate the rocket's expected flight performance and stability.
  • Configured the H283ST-14A motor for the planned flight using a Universal Delay Drill Tool.
  • Completed a launch as part of an L1 rocketry certification attempt.
  • L1 certification status: In progress.
